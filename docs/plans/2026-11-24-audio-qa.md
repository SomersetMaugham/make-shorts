# Plan: automatic narration phrasing QA (2026-11-24)

Goal (the user's): the narration review needs as little human intervention as possible; the user approves recording
once and listens once. Background and the design this plan implements: `/Users/admin/project/shorts/audio-qa-consult/answer.md`
(agy Neon high's proposal; sections 2-4). The user approved on 2026-11-24, **each with a hard cap of 3 tries**:
(1) automatic phrasing re-takes after one recording approval, (2) STT checks for garbled or missing words and an
audio model judging emphasis, (3) code setting paragraph splits and silence cuts inside the loop.

## Hard limits (never exceeded; every loop stops and reports what is left)
- `MAX_RETAKES_PER_PARAGRAPH = 3` automatic re-takes of one paragraph per recording approval.
- `MAX_LOOP_ROUNDS = 3` rounds of the whole audit → fix → re-take loop per short.
- `MAX_CRITIC_RUNS = 3` audio-critic runs per short per recording approval.
- The counters live in `narration/<sk>/qa_state.json` (`{"approval": "<iso time>", "retakes": {"<paragraph key>": n},
  "rounds": n, "critic_runs": n}`), reset only by a new `tts.mjs --approved` started by a person (flag `--new-approval`).

## Components (all in the skill: `scripts/` and `scripts/narration/`)
1. `scripts/prosody_prep.py` — before recording. Uses `kiwipiepy` (optional import: when it is missing, print one
   warning and fall back to today's behaviour). For every cue: (a) sanitize `say` only: remove `"` `'` `“ ” ‘ ’`;
   join spaced decimals `X 점 Y` → `X쩜Y` (Hangul numerals); (b) classify every boundary between eojeol as REQUIRED,
   FORBIDDEN or OPTIONAL by the rules R1-R5 and F1-F8 of answer.md §2 Rank 1 (thresholds as written there);
   (c) insert `, [pause short]` at REQUIRED boundaries that have no pause tag yet; (d) never join words before
   recording except decimals; (e) write `narration/<sk>/prosody_map.json`:
   `{"<cue id>": {"say": "...", "boundaries": [{"after_word": "...", "index": i, "kind": "REQUIRED|FORBIDDEN|OPTIONAL", "rule": "R1"}]}}`.
   Respect `"noPause": true` (line) and `"autoPause": false` (short). Called from `narration_init.py` right after the
   existing auto-pause step, for new or reworded lines only (same condition as today).
   Also pre-recording warnings (printed, not blocking): a cue of 6 syllables or fewer standing alone (suggest merging
   it into the next cue), a one-syllable verb in adnominal form (VV+ETM such as 잰, 잴), and `아니라,` in a cue over 30 syllables.
2. `scripts/narration/phrasing_audit.py` — after recording. Reads `<dir>/phrasing.json` (written by
   `phrasing-check.mjs`; read the file to see its structure) and `prosody_map.json`. For every cue it aligns the heard
   pieces to the words of `say` (normalise: drop spaces, punctuation and tags; compare Hangul by jamo; digits and Latin
   letters as heard are matched to their spoken Hangul through a small table) and reports:
   `MISSING_BREAK` (a REQUIRED boundary or comma/tag with no pause ≥ 0.15 s), `FORBIDDEN_BREAK` (a pause ≥ 0.15 s at a
   FORBIDDEN boundary), `GARBLE` (`<0x..>` bytes in the heard text, or a word of `say` with no match), `BOUNDARY`
   (the cue's heard text does not start with its own first word or end with its own last word: a wrong split).
   Output `<dir>/qa_audit.json` and a printed table; exit 1 when anything is found.
3. `scripts/narration/qa_loop.py` — the loop, run by the agent after the person's approval:
   round = 1..MAX_LOOP_ROUNDS:
     a. `phrasing-check.mjs` (all cues) → `phrasing_audit.py`.
     b. BOUNDARY → move that paragraph's split into the silence between the two words (from the phrasing pieces'
        times) and write `"splits"`; re-run tts.mjs (no new request: same take).
     c. FORBIDDEN_BREAK → join the two words in `say` (remove the space); MISSING_BREAK → insert `, [pause short]`;
        GARBLE on a one-syllable word → print it for the agent (no automatic rewording).
     d. re-take every changed paragraph with `tts.mjs --approved --only <ids>` unless its counter is at
        MAX_RETAKES_PER_PARAGRAPH (then leave it and report).
     e. drafts the cuts with `autocut.py` (interior silence threshold -45 dB instead of -50) and applies them.
   Stops when the audit is clean, a round changes nothing, or the round cap is hit. Prints a final report: what was
   fixed, what is left, the counters.
4. `scripts/narration/audio_critic.py` — optional, after the loop: sends `audio/narration.wav` plus the cue list with
   times to Gemini Neon through agy (`~/.local/bin/agy -p … --model gemini-4-neon-medium`, read-only, the polite
   unambiguous English of CLAUDE.md) asking only for emphasis or intonation that sounds wrong (one-word lines read
   theatrically, a stressed topic, a question read flat) with the cue id and time; writes `<dir>/qa_critic.json`. At
   most MAX_CRITIC_RUNS per approval. It never changes anything: the agent decides.
5. `references/narration.md` — a new section "Automatic phrasing QA (2026-11-24)" describing the policy, the limits
   and the commands, and amending the old rules it relaxes (re-takes after one approval; STT for garbles; code-set
   splits and cuts inside the loop). `scripts/narration/autocut.py`: interior threshold -45 dB.
